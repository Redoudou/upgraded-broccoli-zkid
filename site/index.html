How a check works

An experiment, built after the September 2026 driver's license breach, to show how little it takes to protect people at the door. A bar needs one answer: over 21 or not.

Nothing here is invented. It runs on the identity standards DMVs already issue to: the ISO/IEC 18013-5 mobile driver's license, checked against a DMV issuer trust list, with an open-source zero-knowledge proof on top.

Below: the venue desk and the guest's phone, using the prototype's real flow, copy and timing. Nothing on this page is a real device, wallet or proof.
